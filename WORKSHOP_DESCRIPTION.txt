Title: [B42] Knox Inventory - Tarkov-style UI Overhaul

[h1]Knox Inventory[/h1]
[b]The whole Project Zomboid UI, redesigned in the style of Escape From Tarkov - one stage at a time.[/b]
A grid inventory where every item takes its real space, your character in 3D, and a main menu that is a camp with the heroes of your saves by the fire.

[img]https://images.steamusercontent.com/ugc/17471200081781262748/58D6572D5A1573EC011D4D04610A71F5174049DD/[/img]

[h1]NEW: 1.0 BETA - a test version[/h1]
[b]This item now has two mods: "Knox Inventory" (0.9.8.7, as before) and "Knox Inventory 1.0 BETA". Enable only one.[/b]
The beta is for trying the new features and reporting bugs, [b]not for a full playthrough[/b] - back up your saves. For an existing save: Load, pick it, Mods, swap Knox Inventory for the beta.
[list]
[*][b]Rigs[/b]: bags with compartments, bags in bags, a bag size slider (50-200%).
[*][b]A new right-click menu[/b] with groups and 81 icons, a world menu, alarm clock and fluid windows.
[*][b]Car dashboard and car radio[/b] - working, but they will be reworked.
[*]Tab key, Ctrl+S sort, Alt+drag quick slots, scattered loot, bag fill cubes, new item sizes, new game screens polished, music fixes.
[/list]
Not finished yet: the dashboard, some item sizes, bag balance, menu tooltips. No Health tab in 1.0 (it comes in 2.0). Report bugs in the [b]1.0 BETA[/b] discussion with your console.txt and mod list. Full change list: the in-game news and Change Notes.
[h1]Compatibility[/h1]
[b]Viewpoint[/b], [b]OldPoint[/b] and [b]Project Viewpoint QOL[/b] are supported: Knox windows give you the cursor, Viewpoint's loot key opens Knox on what you look at (pick the fridge or freezer with the wheel), its Grab goes through Knox's grids.
Not compatible: Grid Inventory, Inventory Tetris (they replace the same windows).

[h1]Main menu: the camp by the fire[/h1]
[b]The main menu is a 3D camp at night, and the heroes of your saves are sitting in it.[/b]

[img]https://images.steamusercontent.com/ugc/10696727666732072207/A5B199B09497E702C92676AB0CD54BF5932AE77F/[/img]

[h2]Install[/h2]
[b]Requires [url=https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3619862853]ZombieBuddy[/url][/b] - a one-time setup.
[olist]
[*]Install [url=https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3619862853]ZombieBuddy[/url] exactly as its page says (it starts with the game from its launch options).
[*]Subscribe and enable [b]Knox Inventory[/b], then restart the game.
[*]On the first start, and after every update, ZombieBuddy asks to allow Knox's Java part (KnoxInventoryJava.jar) - allow it.
[/olist]
Without ZombieBuddy the mod stays off, keeps the game's own inventory and menu, and tells you what is missing.

[h2]FAQ[/h2]
[list]
[*][b]Why ZombieBuddy?[/b] Knox's fonts, tooltips, cursor, 3D framing, the live character and the menu's camp need Java code that Lua alone can't reach.
[*][b]Only want the camp menu, or only the inventory?[/b] You can have that right now: the "Knox Inventory | Modules" plate at the top of the main menu switches the in-game interface, the main menu and the new game screens on and off separately. Turn off everything but the main menu and you keep just the camp - it stays part of Knox, not a separate mod.
[*][b]Can I re-upload it or parts of it?[/b] No (see below).
[*][b]Existing save?[/b] Yes, add it any time.
[*][b]My hero is a dark figure in the menu?[/b] Load that save once: the hero's look is recorded while you play.
[*][b]Multiplayer?[/b] 0.9.8.7: singleplayer. 1.0 BETA: works on servers, in testing - tell us how it goes.
[*][b]A windowed inventory?[/b] Not planned - the opacity sliders in Settings > Look let you see the world through it.
[*][b]An item's size or sound?[/b] Set your own in Zomboid/Lua/knox_overrides.txt.
[*][b]With Grid Inventory / Inventory Tetris?[/b] No: both replace the same windows.
[*][b]Controller?[/b] Controller and split-screen keep the game's inventory.
[*][b]Found a bug?[/b] Post in Discussions with what you did and your [i]Zomboid\console.txt[/i].
[/list]

Please don't re-upload Knox Inventory or any part of it (code, models, art), here or elsewhere, and don't put it in modpacks. Link to this page instead.

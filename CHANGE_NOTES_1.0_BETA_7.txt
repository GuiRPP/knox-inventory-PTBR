[h1]Knox Inventory 1.0 BETA - update 6: In Your Sights[/h1]
Only "Knox Inventory 1.0 BETA" changes. The released 0.9.8.7 stays as it was.

[b]A new Java part: when ZombieBuddy asks about KnoxInventoryJava.jar, click "Allow".[/b]

Aim at a fridge - and a panel unfolds right beside the crosshair: sausage, two steaks, a cucumber. For those who play with Viewpoint, the panel at its crosshair is now in Knox's style. And Knox's settings window is made over.

[h2]The crosshair panel (needs Viewpoint)[/h2]
[list]
[*]Works only together with the Viewpoint mod (and OldPoint on top of it). Without it nothing changes.
[*]A header as Knox's menu in the world has: the container's picture and its name. Things of one kind - one row with a number.
[*]The chosen row grows whole: its tile, picture and name get bigger. A long name slides along.
[*]You see the taking: the row fills from left to right, the tile gets a sand frame, a taken row folds smoothly away.
[*]Knox's search right in the panel: while you look at a container, your character searches it, and what is found unfolds into the list. Look away - the search waits.
[*]Bags in a container: a short press of "Take" looks inside, another folds it. Hold it - and you take the whole bag.
[*]Several containers in one thing (a fridge and its freezer) - in sections of their own.
[*]Doors, windows and Viewpoint's other actions are here too; the dangerous ones (smash, break) come last and in red.
[*]The keys are the ones you set in Viewpoint's options; the wheel chooses a row.
[*]The panel changes its width and height smoothly; the count of things rolls like a drum, the hints at the foot change softly.
[*]Don't like it? Turn off "Own Viewpoint crosshair panel" on the "Loot and bags" page of Knox's settings, and Viewpoint's own panel comes back.
[*]If anything goes wrong in the panel, Knox writes one line into console.txt and gives Viewpoint's panel back by itself.
[/list]

[h2]The settings window[/h2]
[list]
[*]It no longer runs off the screen on small monitors.
[*]The settings are laid out on pages: Modules, Look, Loot and bags, Order, Sound, Controls, Main menu.
[*]New pages Sound and Main menu; inside the pages - sections with captions.
[*]It scrolls with the wheel, the slider and a click on the track; the list's edges fade softly.
[/list]

[/list]

[h1]Knox Inventory 1.0 BETA - update 11: Winter Is Coming[/h1]
Only "Knox Inventory 1.0 BETA" changes. The released 0.9.8.7 stays as it was.

[b]A new Java part: when ZombieBuddy asks about KnoxInventoryJava.jar, click "Allow".[/b]

[i]"Nine days now, the long road had been taking us north. Then a sudden blizzard changed our route for us, and we had to take cover in the nearest bar. We knocked the door out, hinges and all, and only once we got our breath back did we see there was nothing left to shut it with. We meant to sit out one night, but we have been stuck in this hole far too long. The first of us found comfort at the bottom of a bottle. The second smokes at a table and cuts notches into the wall - by now he has fewer cigarettes than notches. The third stands at the broken door every morning, looks out through the snow, and can't bring himself to go."[/i]

As for the fourth, search all you like, you won't find them in this room. They don't smoke, don't sleep, and don't speak to anyone.

[h2]Important[/h2]
The bar needs the mod [b]"6282 3D models for Viewpoint" [sour_kisel][/b] (Workshop 3810302175): the tables and chairs come from it. Without it the menu stays at the campfire, and everything else works as usual.

[h2]Magazines[/h2]
Open this update's news in the game: its inventory grid and reload menu are the real, live ones - try them right there.
[list]
[*]A magazine no longer tells you at once how many rounds it holds: now you have to find out, and until then it's hatched. To check a magazine in the inventory, put the mouse on it and press Shift.
[*]To load a magazine, now just drag rounds onto it.
[*]The tile's fill shows about how many rounds the magazine has left.
[*]Hold the reload key, and instead of the usual radial menu Knox's menu opens, with all the magazines you have and about how many rounds are in them. The wheel chooses, the left button does it, the right button loads the chosen magazine, a double right click unloads it.
[/list]
